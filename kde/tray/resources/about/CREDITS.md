## Foundation

**DwarFS** was created by
[Marcus Holland-Moritz and contributors](https://github.com/mhx/dwarfs).
DwarFSctl builds and ships checksum-pinned DwarFS 0.15.10 libraries.

**Qt 6, KDE Frameworks 6, Plasma, and Dolphin** provide the native desktop
platform.

## Libraries and build tools

DwarFSctl also relies on Boost, nlohmann JSON, toml++, range-v3,
parallel-hashmap, utf8cpp, libarchive, OpenSSL, libfuse, compression libraries,
CMake, and Ninja.

Complete license and source notices are included with each package.
